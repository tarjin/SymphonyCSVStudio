Piano Roll staff placement is automatic: C4 (MIDI 60) and higher → Upper / Treble; B3 (59) and lower → Lower / Bass. No configuration needed. Original pitches and timing are unchanged.

All notes, one sound: this merges every MIDI channel into one Piano Roll CSV, including any percussion. You will hear them together through one selected SoundFont; original instrument and drum sounds are not retained. Original MIDI instrument, program, and channel assignments are intentionally discarded in the converted CSV. The Studio track SoundFont determines the sound; you can change it later.
