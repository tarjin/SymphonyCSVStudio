What about SoundFonts I import myself? This page covers only the six SoundFonts supplied with SymphonyCSV Studio, not SoundFonts in your own collection. Third-party SoundFonts may have different terms, especially when you share a Project ZIP containing them. Check the original license before redistributing any SoundFont.
About these licenses: The linked original license and readme files govern the respective SoundFonts. CC0, CC BY 4.0, and the GeneralUser GS License are different agreements; they are not interchangeable. In particular, the MuldjordKit license requires attribution, and the GeneralUser GS distribution terms and source notes deserve careful review.
