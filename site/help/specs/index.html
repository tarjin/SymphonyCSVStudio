Studio LABS · Checkpoint 072

Write the music. Understand every column.

Three exact, version-checked CSV references for musicians and AI assistants. Compose parts in a spreadsheet or text editor, then import them into Studio.

Download the three blank templates (.zip)

Blank templates are not songs: each CSV contains one header row and no events. Complete the required music, tempo, meter and timing values before importing. The newer per-event stereo-panning fields are optional; legacy layouts remain supported by LABS.

Open musical formats

The SymphonyCSV musical CSV formats are openly documented and freely available for anyone to use and implement. Musicians, software developers, researchers and AI systems may create, read, edit and exchange compatible musical files without paying format licensing fees. SymphonyCSV Studio is free to use, while its application source code remains proprietary. Read our format-use policy.

For musicians and AI assistants

Use the exact header spellings and order supplied in the templates. Do not silently add, remove, rename or reorder columns. CSV files use UTF-8 and ordinary quoted CSV cells. Keep the human composer's musical intent authoritative.

Use the sample event files to see valid input. The data dictionary contains every field and guidance for empty cells; the Studio LABS Rust/WASM validator is the final authority on acceptance. The field explanations from the November 19 developer draft have been retained and reconciled to LABS headers; where only examples rather than exhaustive validation are known, they are labelled as guidance rather than guarantees.

For generative workflows, retrieve the machine-readable AI index and the complete per-format field dictionaries.
