For musicians and AI assistants

Use the exact header spellings and order supplied in the templates. Do not silently add, remove, rename or reorder columns. CSV files use UTF-8 and ordinary quoted CSV cells. Keep the human composer's musical intent authoritative.

Use the sample event files to see valid input. The data dictionary contains every field and guidance for empty cells; the Studio RC1 Rust/WASM validator is the final authority on acceptance. The field explanations from the November 19 developer draft have been retained and reconciled to RC1 headers; where only examples rather than exhaustive validation are known, they are labelled as guidance rather than guarantees.

For generative workflows, retrieve the machine-readable AI index and the complete per-format field dictionaries.
