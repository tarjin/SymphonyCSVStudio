Format-use policy · Version 1.0 · 24 November 2026

Open musical formats

The SymphonyCSV musical CSV formats are openly documented and freely available for anyone to use and implement.

Open formats. Free application. Proprietary engineering.

Explore the CSV specifications

1. Scope

This policy applies to the publicly documented SymphonyCSV Piano Roll / Pitched CSV, Guitar TAB CSV and Drums CSV musical file formats, including their published field definitions, required headers and blank templates. It does not apply to the Studio application source code or third-party assets.

2. Royalty-free format use and independent implementation

SymphonyCSV Studio grants everyone permission, worldwide and without format licensing fees or royalties, to use these published musical CSV formats and implement them independently in software, services and workflows, for personal, educational, research, nonprofit or commercial purposes. No individual permission or registration is required.

Musicians, developers, researchers and AI systems may create, read, edit, validate, convert, exchange and distribute compatible musical files, and develop and distribute independent software that supports the formats. The permission covers using the published field names, required structure and blank headers to achieve interoperability. No fee is due to SymphonyCSV Studio merely for exercising these permissions.

3. Free application; proprietary source

SymphonyCSV Studio is free to use. Its application source code and proprietary implementation are not licensed by this format-use policy. Independent implementation does not require access to that source code. This is a format-use permission, not an open-source software license or a blanket license to copy the website, documentation, branding or other copyrighted material.

4. Your music and third-party rights

Using the formats does not transfer ownership of your musical works to SymphonyCSV Studio. You remain responsible for rights in the music, recordings and other material you create, import or distribute. SoundFonts, samples and other third-party assets retain their own licenses. The format-use permission does not grant rights in third-party material.

5. Compatibility and naming

You may accurately describe independently developed software as supporting a SymphonyCSV format. Do not imply official certification, sponsorship or endorsement, or use SymphonyCSV Studio branding in a misleading way. Consult the versioned specifications and test compatibility with the intended Studio release. These are publicly documented formats, not a claim of ISO or other formal standards-body certification.

6. Technical reference and evolution

Consult the published specifications for current headers, field meanings, examples and blank templates. The online Product & Functional Reference explains the wider application. Format versions may evolve; the applicable version and actual compatibility should be checked. This permission does not guarantee that every implementation or file will be accepted by every Studio version.

Policy effective 24 November 2026. This published permission clarifies the earlier reference's proposed format-use position; it does not change the LABS application or CSV schemas.
