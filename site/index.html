What can you make?

One song idea. Several possibilities.

Start by building a song demo. When you're ready, turn your musical parts into sheet music or take them into your favourite recording environment.

WORKFLOW 01

Make a song demo

Build piano, guitar, bass and drum parts. Add recorded audio, balance your tracks, adjust panning and effects, then export a mix to share with other musicians.

Compose → Arrange → Mix → Export
WORKFLOW 02

Create sheet music

Export a musical track as MIDI and open it in MuseScore Studio. Review the generated notation, make adjustments where needed, and prepare printable sheet music.

Compose → MIDI → MuseScore → Score
WORKFLOW 03

Finish it in a DAW

Develop your arrangement in SymphonyCSV Studio, then export MIDI or audio for use in REAPER or another DAW. Record, produce, and polish your final release there.

Demo → Export → DAW → Production

A different approach to songwriting

Describe the music. Hear it come alive.

SymphonyCSV Studio works with structured music files called SymphonyCSV. They describe notes, rhythms, instruments and performance details using an open CSV format.

Write those files yourself or use an AI assistant to help build your musical parts. You can also convert compatible MIDI files into SymphonyCSV.

Import your tracks, listen to them together, adjust the mix and export your demo.

You don't need a traditional graphical note editor to start making music. Your composition remains readable, editable and portable.
